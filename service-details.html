What we check

A careful inspection before adjustment.

We inspect tyre wear and steering concerns, then measure wheel angles against the vehicle's alignment specifications. If adjustment is appropriate, we explain the findings and get your approval before proceeding.

  • Visual tyre and steering inspection
  • Measurement of wheel alignment angles
  • Adjustment where supported by vehicle specifications
  • Post-service review of the results

When to book a check

Consider an inspection if your vehicle pulls to one side, the steering wheel is off-center, tyres show uneven wear, or suspension work has recently been completed.

Typical time: about 45 minutes
Starting price: $79
