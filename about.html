About TORQ

Built around safer roads and smarter choice.

TORQ is a premium tyre and wheel care studio helping drivers make better decisions for safety, comfort, and performance.

Our story

A workshop born from precision, trust, and performance.

TORQ started with a simple idea: every driver deserves expert guidance, accurate diagnostics, and premium equipment without inflated pressure.

From everyday family cars to luxury performance machines, we approach each tyre and alignment service with a genuine passion for vehicles.

Our mission

Make vehicle care clearer and safer.

We help drivers choose appropriate tyres and services through honest advice, careful workmanship, and straightforward explanations.

Our vision

Be the workshop drivers return to.

We aim to build lasting trust through consistent standards, thoughtful recommendations, and respect for every vehicle and budget.

Our standard

Quality you can understand.

From fitment checks to alignment reports, we explain what we find and what we recommend before carrying out additional work.

Our people

Care from people who put safety first.

Our workshop is built around clear advice, careful checks, and respect for every driver.
